## Weapon Collection

![Weapon collection](resource:assets/markdown/en/weaponCollectionScreen_overview.png)

A built-in database of preset rifles and pistols. Tap the **check-mark button** (bottom-right of a card) to take that weapon for the profile.

Each card shows:
- **Manufacturer** and **model name**
- **Caliber** and **barrel length**
- **Twist rate** and twist direction (right/left)

---

### Search

Type in the search field to filter the list by name or manufacturer. The list updates instantly.

---

### Filter

Tap the **filter** icon (top-right) to open the Filter sheet and narrow the list by **Vendor**. Choose **Apply** to use it or **Reset** to clear it. A badge on the icon shows an active filter.
