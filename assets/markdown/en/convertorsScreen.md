## Convertors

![Convertors](resource:assets/markdown/en/convertorsScreen_grid.png)

A set of tools for quick conversion of ballistic and physical quantities.

Tap any tile to open a convertor: **Target Distance**, **Velocity**, **Length**, **Weight**, **Pressure**, **Temperature**, **Angles** and **Torque**. Each remembers the last value and unit you used.

---

### Simple Convertors

Enter a value in the top field and select its unit — all other values update instantly.

| Convertor | Units |
|---|---|
| **Length** | cm, m, inch, foot, yard |
| **Weight** | g, kg, grain, pound, ounce |
| **Pressure** | hPa, mmHg, inHg, psi, bar, atm |
| **Temperature** | °C, °F |
| **Torque** | N·m, ft·lb, in·lb |

---

### Advanced Convertors

Three convertors have extra fields and their own guide (the **?** button):

- **Velocity** — convert between m/s, km/h, fps, mph and Mach number, with a custom atmosphere option
- **Target Distance** — calculate distance from a target's angular and physical size
- **Angles** — convert between angular units and compute adjustments at a given distance
