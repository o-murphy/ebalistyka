## Cartridge / Bullet Collection

![Cartridge collection](resource:assets/markdown/en/ammoCollectionScreen_overview.png)

Two built-in databases: the **Cartridge Collection** (ready-made cartridges) and the **Bullet Collection** (projectiles only). When you replace a profile's cartridge you choose **Select cartridge from collection** or **Select bullet from collection**; after you pick one the cartridge editor opens with its values filled in, so you can add the missing data (such as the muzzle velocity for a bullet) and confirm.

Tap the **check-mark button** (bottom-right of a card) to choose that item.

Each card shows:
- **Manufacturer** and the **cartridge** (or bullet) name, with the **projectile name** under it
- **G1 BC** and **G7 BC** — the primary drag model is highlighted
- **Muzzle velocity**
- **Caliber**, **projectile weight** and **projectile length**

---

### Search

Type in the search field to filter by name, manufacturer or projectile name. The list updates instantly.

---

### Filter

![Filter](resource:assets/markdown/en/ammoCollectionScreen_filter.png)

Tap the **filter** icon (top-right) to open the Filter sheet. Expand a group to narrow the list by:
- **Vendor**
- **Caliber**
- **Weight** — a range of projectile weights

**Apply** uses the filter, **Reset** clears it. A badge on the icon shows an active filter.

---

### Caliber pre-filter

When you open the collection from a profile whose weapon is already set, the list is pre-filtered to the matching caliber. You can remove this in the Filter sheet.
