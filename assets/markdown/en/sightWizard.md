## New Sight / Edit Sight

![Sight editor](resource:assets/markdown/en/sightWizard_overview.png)

Fill in the fields to create a scope (sight) or edit the scope of a profile. It opens when you add or replace the scope of a profile, or tap the pencil next to **Sight** on the Profiles screen. Tap **Confirm** to save or **Discard** to leave without saving; the scope needs a name before it can be saved.

The picture at the top (**Sight Image**) is a placeholder for a photo of the scope — pictures aren't supported yet. It has nothing to do with the reticle, which is chosen below.

---

### General

- **Sight name** — a label to identify this scope (required)
- **Vendor** — manufacturer name

---

### Mounting

- **Sight height** — distance from the bore axis to the optical axis of the scope; affects the elevation calculation
- **Horizontal Offset** — lateral displacement of the scope from the bore centre; leave at 0 for centre-mounted optics

---

### Reticle

- **Reticle pattern** — tap to choose the reticle image shown on the Reticle screen
- **FFP / SFP / LWIR** — the focal plane: First (FFP), Second (SFP), or thermal (LWIR)
- **Min Magnification / Max Magnification** — zoom range of the scope; used together with the focal plane to scale the reticle correctly

---

### Clicks

- **Vertical click / Horizontal click** — angular value of one turret click on each axis; pick the unit (MIL, MOA, …) next to each value
