## New Weapon / Edit Weapon

![Weapon editor](resource:assets/markdown/en/weaponWizard_overview.png)

Fill in the fields to create a weapon or edit the weapon of a profile. It opens when you create a profile, replace the weapon of a profile, or tap the pencil next to **Weapon** on the Profiles screen. Tap **Confirm** to save or **Discard** to leave without saving; the weapon needs a name before it can be saved.

---

### General

- **Weapon name** — a label to identify this weapon (required)
- **Vendor** — manufacturer name
- **Caliber name** — free-text caliber designation (e.g. "6.5 Creedmoor", ".308 Win")

---

### Ballistics

- **Caliber** — actual bullet diameter; used in ballistic calculations. The caliber is shared with the cartridge of the profile — changing it here updates the cartridge as well

---

### Hardware

- **Twist** — barrel rifling pitch (distance per one full rotation, e.g. 1:9.5 inch); affects the stability calculation
- **Twist direction** — right-hand or left-hand rifling
- **Additional parameters** — switch on to show **Barrel length**
